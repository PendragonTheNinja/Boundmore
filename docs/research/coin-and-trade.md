- **Crafter:** recipes handcrafted 100/1,000/10,000/100,000/1,000,000; at a crafting table 100/500/5,000/50,000/500,000; at a furnace, workbench, extractor, mixer, compactor, refinery 100/500/1,000/10,000/100,000 each; unique recipes crafted 20/50/100/200/500.
- **Explorer:** distance sprinted 1,000/10,000/100,000/1,000,000/10,000,000; crept 50/500/10,000/100,000/1,000,000; swum 1,000 to 10,000,000 (as sprinted); climbed 1,000/10,000/100,000/1,000,000/1,000,000; travelled 10,000 to 100,000,000; worlds discovered 10/25/50/75/100; regions discovered 10/50/100/500/1,000; worlds with every region explored 1/4/7/11/16; coin spent on warps 1,000/10,000/100,000/1,000,000/1,000,000.
- **Gatherer:** trunks, foliage, flowers, plants, fungi, soil, surface boulders gathered 50/500/5,000/50,000/500,000 each; axes and shovels worn out 5/100/300/500/1,000.
- **Miner:** rock mined 100/500/5,000/50,000/500,000; coal, metal ore, ancient technology, fossils, gleam, gems mined 100/1,000/10,000/100,000/1,000,000 each; unique items mined 30/100/200/300/500; hammers worn out 5/100/300/500/1,000.

## Shops and the tax

| Finding | Source | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Plinth: refinery, 2 base metal, 10 stones, 2 glue, 3 min, decoration crafting 1, wear 70 | wiki Plinth | H |
| Shop stand: refinery, 2 storage blocks, a plinth, 2 cotton cloth, 7 min 30 s, spark 200, wear 150, decoration crafting 2; the owner sets an item and price; buyers pay without the owner there | wiki Shop Stand | H |
| Request basket: the same recipe at decoration crafting 3; the owner puts in one of the item (category and colour) to set what is wanted, prices it and charges it with coin; sellers are paid at once; the owner collects the items | wiki Request Basket | H |
| **The tax: 10% of each sale through a stand or basket, paid by the seller, removed from the game** | players t/48091, t/25321, t/30423, t/31272 (2019 to 2020) | H |
| **Tax Epic:** "Reduces the amount of Tax you pay as a Seller by 25%"; it takes effect at a beacon once the seller revisits it with the epic's skill page active | boundlexx skill text; t/30423 | H |
| Players hand-traded (dropped items) to avoid the tax | t/21095, t/34821 | H |
| Setting a price of 0 showed as 1 to buyers (a bug) | t/13212 | M |
| Release 230 added world shop scanners: an in-game device showing the shops of a world (physical finding, not a global index) | t/34821 (Testing 230) | M |
