# Building blocks: research (2026-10-26)

Boundless's crafted building set, from the game's own recipe data as the fan site boundless.mayumi.fi publishes it (`recipes.js`, the recipe list with machine, single-craft quantity, seconds, spark and power), joined with the item names from its `items.msgpack` and the colour rule from the same data's `tintTakenFrom` (which input's colour the output takes). Facts only: Boundmore names its own blocks. Confidence high throughout (the game's data, release 249-era).

## Rules read from the data

- **Colour passes through crafting.** Every building recipe names the input whose colour the output takes (`tintTakenFrom`): rock blocks take the rock's colour (rock → stones → refined → decorative, and brick mixture → bricks), timber takes the trunk's, refined and decorative wood the timber's, glass blocks and panes the refined gleam's, gleam blocks the gleam's, compact soil the soil's, gravel the rock's, sand the gravel's, metal doors, poles, beams and signs **the metal's** (copper, iron, silver, gold and titanium are tints there), stone and wood doors and furniture the stones' or timber's. Metal chain blocks (compact, refined, machined copper) and gem blocks take none: they have one look.
- **Three of each.** Rock blocks come in the three rock kinds (igneous, metamorphic, sedimentary), each with stones, bricks, refined and two decorative blocks; wood in the three trunk kinds with timber, refined and decorative. Marble is metamorphic only. "Any Stones", "Any Timber", "Any Refined Rock" and so on are groups.
- **Tiers of furnishing**: doors, trapdoors, poles, beams, storage and signs come plain, stylish and ornate, in wood, stone, metal and gleam; plain from base stuff at the crafting table or refinery, ornate from refined stuff with power.
